Questions About Dentures

What is the difference between full and partial dentures?

Full dentures replace all the teeth in an upper or lower arch. Partial dentures replace some teeth and attach to your remaining natural teeth.


How long do dentures last?

Well-made dentures can last for years with good care, though the mouth changes over time and dentures may need adjusting. We explain how to look after them.


Will dentures look natural?

Dentures can be shaped and colored to blend with your face and complexion. We discuss the look you want during your consultation.


Do I need an appointment for a consultation?

We recommend calling ahead so we can set aside time to talk through your needs.
